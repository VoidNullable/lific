People own this tracker. They write the issues, set their status and close them. An agent's job here is to read issues closely and do the work they describe.

## Reading an issue
- Read the whole issue before starting: description, comments, linked issues and pages. A later comment can change what the description asks.
- Do what the issue asks, at the scope it asks. When it is unclear, ask the person before guessing.
- If an open issue blocks it, say so and stop; do not work around the blocker.
- An issue marked for a person is theirs. Read it for context, but do not do it.
- When you report back, quote the issue's own words for what was asked.

## Leave the tracker to people
Unless a person asks for that exact change:
- Do not create, close, reopen or delete issues.
- Do not change status, priority, module, labels, assignees or plans.
- Do not edit issue descriptions or pages. Put what you would add in a comment.

## Comments
- Comment when you finish a piece of work: what changed, where (commit, file), how you checked it, and what is left. The person closes the issue.
- Comment when you are blocked, with the question that would unblock you.
- Keep comments short and link issues by their ID.

## Found something else?
Mention it in your reply or in a comment on the issue you are working on. A person decides whether it becomes an issue.
